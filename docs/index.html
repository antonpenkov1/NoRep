NoRep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

NoRep is a workout timer. It is built to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. NoRep does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is no account, no registration, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

Your timer settings and workout history are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network access

NoRep does not connect to the internet.

Children's privacy

Since no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of NoRep changes any of the above (for example, adds optional ads or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy label will reflect the change.
